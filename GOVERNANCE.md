# Governance

rok-db is a community project that currently follows a
**maintainer-led** model.

## Roles

- **Users** use rok-db and are encouraged to report bugs and share feedback.
- **Contributors** have had a pull request, issue triage, review or RFC
  accepted. Anyone can become a contributor.
- **Maintainers** have write access. They review and merge pull requests,
  triage issues, accept RFCs, cut releases and enforce the
  [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Decision making

- Day-to-day decisions (bug fixes, docs, internal refactors) are made in
  pull request review: one maintainer approval and green CI are enough.
- Public API additions, breaking changes, new dependencies, MSRV bumps and new
  supported databases go through the [RFC process](docs/rfcs/README.md). An
  RFC is accepted when maintainers reach consensus after a final comment
  period of at least 7 days. If consensus can't be reached, the lead
  maintainer decides and records the reasoning on the RFC.
- Decisions are made in public (issues, pull requests, RFCs); private
  channels are used only for security and conduct reports.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who have made sustained, high-quality contributions (code,
review or triage) over several months may be nominated by any maintainer.
Nominations are accepted when no existing maintainer objects within 7 days.
Maintainers who are inactive for 12 months may be moved to emeritus status
and can return at any time by asking.

## Changes to this document

Changes to governance follow the RFC process.
